# Changelog

## 0.1.0


### Features

* telemetry from Forza Motorsport and Forza Horizon (Data Out over UDP: sled, dash, Horizon 4/5 and Motorsport 2023 formats), a built-in demo car, and `.ctlog` recording and replay
* 12 dashboard widgets (analog and bar gauges, readouts, gear, shift lights, warning lamps, tyre temperatures, g-meter, pedals, lap timer, labels, images) and four original sample dashboards
* JSON5 dashboard files with validation, hot reload and per-unit metric/imperial display
* visual dashboard editor with undo/redo, snapping, alignment, and `.cluster` package import/export
* phone and tablet display in the browser (WebAssembly), with QR code, and mirroring to other computers on the network
* settings panel and a first-run guide for connecting Forza Horizon 5
